FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Before You Validate Your Idea

Get direct answers to testing early assumptions, reaching appropriate people and gaining helpful input before you create.

What is idea validation?+

Idea validation is the process of evaluating the basic assumptions around a business or startup idea before investing a lot of time or expense. It tells you whether the problem is worth solving, who cares about it, what value people see, and what hazards need further examination.

How does business idea validation work?+

Start by identifying the biggest assumption behind your business idea. Define the audience affected by the problem, explain your idea clearly, and look for consistent feedback rather than relying on a few positive responses.

Can I validate an idea before building an MVP?+

Yes. For an early-stage startup, idea validation can begin before a working product exists. You can validate the problem, audience, current behaviour, and value proposition before development. Getting feedback early allows you to determine what to invest more in.

Can I validate a SaaS idea before building the software?+

Yes. SaaS idea validation might start with evaluating if the desired audience has the problem, what they are using today and whether the offered value is relevant enough to investigate further. These early queries don't require a viable product.

How many people should validate an idea?+

There is no magic number that will prove an idea works. The proper sample depends on who you're talking to and what decision you're making. Start with a relevant group, find recurring signals, and expand the mission when you require stronger evidence.

What's the difference between idea validation and market validation?+

Idea validation is about whether the problem, the audience and the value proposed are worth further investigation. Market validation is a wider view of business demand, alternatives, rivalry and market conditions. The two can be of assistance to each other, yet they answer different questions.
